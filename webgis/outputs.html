Maps & Visualisations

Project Outputs

Seven satellite-derived maps covering the Australian continent. Each layer captures a distinct environmental signal — click any card to expand it full-screen.

Land Cover
MODIS MCD12C1 · 500 m · 2022

🌍 Land Cover 2022

Eight aggregated IGBP classes derived from the MODIS annual land-cover product. Captures the broad ecological zonation of Australia — from tropical forests in the north to arid shrublands in the interior.

Vegetation
MODIS MOD13A2 · 1 km · 2010–2021 mean

🌿 NDVI Mean (2010–2021)

Twelve-year mean of the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index. Bright greens mark dense vegetation while browns indicate bare soil and deserts — revealing the contrast between coastal productive zones and the arid interior.

Temperature
MODIS MOD11A1 · 1 km · 2019 daytime mean

🌡️ Land Surface Temperature 2019

Mean daytime land surface temperature in degrees Celsius. Hotspots reach above 45 °C in the central deserts and the Pilbara, while cooler coastal regions reflect maritime influence and vegetation cover.

Fire
MODIS MCD64A1 · 500 m · 2021

🔥 Burned Area 2021

Maximum burn-date composite for 2021. Colours represent the day of year the fire occurred — pale yellows are early-season burns, deep reds are late-season burns. Reveals the annual fire cycle across northern savannas.

Forest
Hansen GFC v1.11 · 30 m · 2020–2022

🌲 Forest Loss 2020–2022

Stand-replacement forest loss over three years. Concentrations appear along the east coast (NSW, Queensland) and in southwest WA — areas subject to both bushfires and land clearing.

Fire
VIIRS SNPP · 375 m · Oct 2023

🔥 Fire Temperature Classes Oct 2023

Active-fire detections classified by brightness temperature. Five classes — cool (yellow) through extreme (dark red) — highlight the intense fires that occurred across northern Australia in October 2023.
