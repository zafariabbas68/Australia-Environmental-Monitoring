Maps & Visualisations

Project Outputs

Six satellite-derived maps covering the Australian continent. Each layer captures a distinct environmental signal — tap any card to expand it full-screen.

Land Cover
MODIS MCD12C1 · 500 m · 2022

🌍 Land Cover 2022

Eight aggregated IGBP classes derived from the MODIS annual land-cover product. Captures the broad ecological zonation of Australia.

Vegetation
MODIS MOD13A2 · 1 km · 2010–2021

🌿 NDVI Mean (2010–2021)

Twelve-year mean of the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index. Reveals the contrast between coastal productive zones and the arid interior.

Temperature
MODIS MOD11A1 · 1 km · 2019

🌡️ Land Surface Temperature 2019

Mean daytime land surface temperature in °C. Hotspots reach above 45 °C in the central deserts and the Pilbara.

Fire
MODIS MCD64A1 · 500 m · 2021

🔥 Burned Area 2021

Maximum burn-date composite for 2021. Pale yellows are early-season burns, deep reds are late-season. Reveals the annual fire cycle across northern savannas.

Forest
Hansen GFC v1.11 · 30 m · 2020–2022

🌲 Forest Loss 2020–2022

Stand-replacement forest loss over three years. Concentrations appear along the east coast (NSW, Queensland) and in southwest WA.

Fire
VIIRS SNPP · 375 m · Oct 2023

🔥 Fire Temperature Classes Oct 2023

Active-fire detections classified by brightness temperature. Five classes — cool (yellow) through extreme (dark red).
